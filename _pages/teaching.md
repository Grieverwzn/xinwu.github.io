## INFORM Africa: Human Mobility & Infectious Disease Dynamics {#inform-africa}

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/teaching/inform_africa_logo.png' | relative_url }}" alt="INFORM Africa logo" style="max-width: 260px;">

[INFORM Africa](https://inform-africa.org/) (*Role of Data Streams in Informing Infection Dynamics in Africa*) is an NIH-funded research hub that uses big data from **Nigeria and South Africa** to strengthen pandemic preparedness for SARS-CoV-2 and HIV. I serve as **Project Manager of Project 2**, led by Dr. Chenfeng Xiong (Villanova University), which links mobile-phone mobility data with COVID-19 risk.

Together with Dr. Xiong, I developed and presented a **six-lecture training package** that introduces human mobility analysis for infectious disease dynamics:

- theoretical foundations of human mobility and epidemics
- big data sources for mobility analysis (LBS, mobile-phone data)
- fundamental mobility metrics and concepts
- geospatial visualization tools
- analytical methods linking travel behavior to infection patterns
- empirical cases from African countries and our published research

The package is open to researchers, public health professionals, epidemiologists and policymakers.
