I grew up in **Kunming (昆明)**, the "Spring City" of Yunnan (云南), next to the former campus of National Southwest Associated University (西南联大). My family's roots are in **Heijing (黑井)**, an old salt town in central Yunnan. I studied by the sea in Dalian and in Beijing before moving to the United States, where my own family has grown.
